A lending library.

Donate kitchenware and tools. Borrow what is available, return what is lent, and keep the story in the record.

Open library
Donate a thing
Borrow and return
Keep its story

Member interface: join with the invitation link the library’s staff gave you, then donate, borrow and return.

Published shelf: public pages show each thing and its story.

Public history: a published copy of the library’s story.

Member journal: in the member interface, after you join. It is the authority for acts that took effect and who signed them. The published history is a copy.

Membership is by invitation. Shelf pages and public history are public. The journal is for members. Only staff can cancel a thing.
