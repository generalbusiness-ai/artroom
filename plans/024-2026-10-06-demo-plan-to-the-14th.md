of plan 019 with the rules refusal of plan 016, ending with the verifier.

that closes the issue; a source reviewer approves and a change to the
rules extent is refused until the rules scope's controller lands it;
merge closes the issue; the verifier replays the room. The rules are
shown to be the room's own. Everything else is a slide or a short local
clip.
