## 1. Revised sequence and complete experience

First specify and deliver one compelling complete GitHub-style demo.
Derive its reusable composable authority architecture. Explicitly retarget
all other applications and components and remove superseded architecture.
The demo alone cannot close this workstream.

The demo's issue is to add project export. It splits endpoint, browser and
integration/documentation concerns. It includes an interface question,
handover, exact contribution reports, a failing combined check despite
passing child checks, independent review and revision, a changed destination
base, a lost push reply and confirmed publication of the exact deployable
Git result. An unrelated issue progresses during delays. Issue, work and
PR are lane forms carrying the full work conversation, not an execution-only
lease with separate product objects.

The person starts coding on A, leaves it, returns as the same member on
B, watches and steers the same work, answers attention, reviews real files
and follows authoritative outcomes. Unauthorized access is refused.
Revocation, pause/cancellation, interruption and unknown effects have
explicit outcomes. These useful [005 browser outcomes](005-2026-10-03-browser-cloud-work.md)
are retained for reassessment; their old Room, identity, storage and
control mechanisms may be replaced.

No Artroom backward compatibility is required: no legacy decoder,
API/client adapter, old identity map, dual fold/publication architecture,
prior engine or pre-change replay. Historical Git/workroom records remain
evidence. Exact subjects, causal provenance, deterministic replay and
recoverable effects within the chosen model remain correctness requirements.
Breaking pre-production APIs and fresh deployment/data choices are available.
Real outstanding effects and credentials must be settled or explicitly
accounted for before resources are reset or retired.

The 10× test-economy goal remains useful. Old integration, old-format
fixtures and previous readiness sequencing do not remain mandatory solely
because they were commissioned. Reassess them against the demo-first
model. Production rollout implementation is excluded. Woo, atseq and the
gitseq tracking service are outside these changes.
