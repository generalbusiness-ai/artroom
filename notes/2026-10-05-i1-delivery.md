## 4. What it costs to check

Two observed runs, one each, on the same shared 18-core machine with other
sessions active, each with a fresh `npm ci` from a warm package cache.

| Step | Main at `b2a0bb12`, the earlier product | This branch at `c2ea17ce` |
|---|---|---|
| install | 4.9 s elapsed, 9.9 s CPU | 2.0 s elapsed, 2.6 s CPU |
| typecheck | 6.4 s elapsed, 13.2 s CPU | 1.7 s elapsed, 3.3 s CPU |
| test | 27.5 s elapsed, 61.3 s CPU | 3.6 s elapsed, 5.5 s CPU |
| tests run | 2,060 | 201 |

**These two columns do not measure the same product.** The left column
checks the whole earlier product: the Room, Git effects, checkers, the
command line, the tool endpoint and the browser pages. The right column
checks the scope substrate only. The lane definitions, authority,
publication, agents and the application are not built yet, and their tests
will add to the right column. So the table shows what a change to the
substrate costs to check today. It is not a tenfold gain, and no such claim
is made. A like-for-like figure needs the later deliveries.

What can be said for the test economy rules: pure derivation is tested once,
in `derive`; the real storage boundary, the real transport and the real
service binding are each shown in a handful of tests; and no test was ported
from the earlier packages to keep a count.

## 5. Where the source goes beyond the adopted contract

`notes/2026-10-04-i1-contract-deltas.md` lists 152 places where the contract
was silent or needed a concrete form, or the source was corrected, with what
was implemented. Entries 1 to 114 are disposed of in revision 10, the adopted
contract. Entries 115 to 150 are owed to its successor (request `108ea663`).
Entry 151 changes no rule, and entry 152 extends entry 143 and goes with it.
None is adopted by being implemented. The ones that most affect behaviour:

- Timed rules, the `rules` member, the derived `Effect` list, and named
  refusal reasons are added to the contract's types.
- Capacity is counted by admitted duty, with one closing checkpoint and
  with each request reserving what its clauses can start. A definition
  whose timed rules form a cycle is refused `reserve-unbounded`.
- A child reads its declared definition's bytes by digest from its creator.
- Preparation runs outside the turn's queue.

## 7. Known shortfalls in what is delivered

- The reserve for a request is one definition-wide maximum, never less than
  that request's own closure. It can refuse work that would have fitted.
- A `conflict` result waits for a free entry rather than drawing on a
  reservation.
- The turn's handling of a timed draft that does not fit has no witness: no
  validated definition reaches it.
- The `where` index on item slots is written and not yet read.
- Retransmission does not resume after a diagnosis.
- Six short names each mean two shapes in different packages: `Clock`,
  `Transport`, `Signer`, `Fetch`, `Page`, `Stored`.
- The `artroom-replay` executable was run under Node 26.10.0 only. Its
  declared range is `^22.18.0 || >=23.6.0`.
- The rule evaluator is the earlier restricted evaluator, copied after
  review. Its earlier test corpus was not rerun against the copy.
- Every number in `Bounds` is the contract's proposal or a temporary value,
  labelled so in the source.
